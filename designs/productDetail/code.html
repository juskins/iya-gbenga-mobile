The Real Taste of Eastern Nigeria: Untouched, Aromatic, and Velvety Thick

Iya Gbenga's Original Nsukka Palm Oil is famously celebrated for its deep wine-red coloration, characteristic nutty sweetness, and exceptionally low moisture content. Sourced directly from palm groves in the rolling hills of Enugu State, this oil settles naturally without separation, synthetic thickeners, or color enhancers.

When fried or heated, it creates an irreplaceable aromatic fragrance that forms the backbone of authentic traditional Nigerian delicacies. Because it contains zero water dilution, a single ladle goes twice as far as typical open-market repackaged oils.

Keeping Your Palm Oil Fresh & Fragrant

Authentic palm oil has a natural shelf life of up to 18 months when protected from moisture and direct sunlight. Because our oil is unadulterated, partial solidification may occur during cooler Harmattan seasons or air-conditioned pantries; this is a hallmark of natural palmitic fatty acids and does not affect quality or flavor.
