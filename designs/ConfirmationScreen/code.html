Mama Gbenga’s Storage & Care Advice

Pro Tips

Make your authentic Nigerian foodstuffs last 3x longer without mold or loss of natural flavor.

inventory_2 How to store Benue Yam Tubers so they never rot or dry out expand_more
Keep the whole tubers in a cool, dark, well-aerated pantry or basket off the bare concrete floor (place on cardboard or dry wood). Do not wash with water until the exact day you prepare to peel, boil, or pound! If partially cut, sprinkle lime juice or light salt over the exposed white flesh and wrap snugly in brown paper.
water_drop Nsukka Unadulterated Palm Oil Preservation expand_more
Store tightly capped in a shaded cupboard away from direct tropical sunlight. Never insert a wet spoon or scoop into the 5-liter keg. Our palm oil is 100% natural, freshly pressed with zero chemical coloring or paraffin; if the bottom solidifies during chilled weather, simply set the keg in lukewarm water for 10 minutes.
set_meal Oron Smoked Crayfish Shelf-Life Extension expand_more
Our Oron crayfish arrives sun-dried and clay-oven smoked to crispy perfection. Keep in the airtight jar provided. For extended 6-month storage, portion into zip-lock pouches and store in the freezer compartment. It grinds directly from frozen without clumping!
